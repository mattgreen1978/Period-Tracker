Back up your data regularly, especially before updating the app or changing phones. Backups and CSV files contain sensitive health information and are not encrypted.

Privacy

Records are saved in this browser's local storage. They are not sent to a server by this app. This version has no Face ID or passcode lock. Anyone with access to this unlocked browser may be able to view the data. Use with each person's knowledge and consent. Clearing website data may permanently erase records unless you have a backup.
